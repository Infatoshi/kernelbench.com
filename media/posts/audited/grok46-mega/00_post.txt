Grok 4.6 wrote an insane megakernel. 21x the PyTorch baseline.

It was tested on: Kimi-Linear W4A16 batch-1 decode for RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell.

> Fable 5 at 24.6x
> Kimi K3 at 18.1x
> GPT-5.6 Sol at 2.6x
> Grok 4.5 at 0.82x

One cooperative CUDA launch per token. 33 grid.sync phases. Packed int4 GEMV. Absorbed MLA. Idle CTAs prefetch the next phase. The data_ptr check only skips copying a cache it already owns. Same continuation pattern as Fable.

I have used Grok 4.6. So far it's been quite reliable. Sometimes it'll tell me I'm right about optimizations. Generally it's competent on the direction to push, and it doesn't get stuck in loops or take a bad direction. Grok is also really fast compared to all other models.
